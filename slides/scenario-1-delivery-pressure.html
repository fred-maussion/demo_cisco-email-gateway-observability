Scenario 01 · lead story

Detect delivery pressure before users complain.

The customer wants earlier warning when email still enters the gateway, but delivery quality starts to degrade.

Pain

Delivery issues surface through tickets.

By the time users report delayed or rejected email, the operations team already works from a reactive position.

Elastic approach

Correlate mail flow, queue pressure, and gateway health.

Elastic shows the signals that indicate delivery pressure and gives the administrator filters to narrow the scope.
