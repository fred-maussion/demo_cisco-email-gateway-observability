Scenario 02 · domain scope

Identify whether the issue affects one destination domain.

The customer wants to understand whether delivery problems come from the gateway fleet or from a specific external destination.

Pain

One partner domain reports delayed or rejected email.

Without domain-level visibility, the team spends time proving whether the problem is internal or external.

Elastic approach

Compare delivery and bounce signals by domain.

Elastic lets the administrator filter by destination domain and compare delivery, hard bounces, and soft bounces.
