Scenario 03 · capacity pressure

Connect queue growth with gateway capacity.

The customer wants to know whether delivery pressure comes from traffic volume, queue buildup, or host resource drift.

Pain

Traffic peaks can hide emerging capacity limits.

Mail may still move, but rising queues and resource drift can signal a future service risk.

Elastic approach

Track queues beside host and system metrics.

Elastic links queue trends with CPU, RAM, disk I/O, system utilization, SMTP connections, and latest host status.

Benefit

Support capacity planning with operational evidence.
